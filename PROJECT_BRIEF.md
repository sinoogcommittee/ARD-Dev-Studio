# Portfolio Website Brief

## Goal

Create a visually memorable personal portfolio for Anthony Riel C. Diango that presents his live software projects as the main attraction. The site should help individuals, agencies, businesses, and hiring teams understand what he builds and contact him about a website, web app, system, or role.

## Audience and desired action

- Primary audience: prospective clients and employers reviewing project work.
- Main action: open a live project or contact Riel to discuss an opportunity.
- Secondary action: view his experience, technical stack, and professional background.

## Product requirements

- A confident landing section with his name, full-stack role, concise positioning, and direct contact/project calls to action.
- A browsable, filterable project gallery featuring all 16 deployments in `CONTENT.md`; project cards prioritize purpose, category, stack, and live link.
- Featured projects should span business, civic/tourism, events, and education rather than making the page feel like a single-domain portfolio.
- About/experience, technical skills, education and credentials, availability, and contact sections.
- Contact options for email, phone, and Facebook, as supplied in `CONTENT.md`.
- Rich but purposeful motion: an engaging opening, restrained transitions, and scroll reveals. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Responsive, accessible, fast-loading presentation with project links and contact information easy to find.

## Stack and delivery

- Editor: VS Code
- UI: React and Tailwind CSS
- Backend service: Supabase, used only where it adds value (such as an optional contact form).
- Preserve a working email/phone contact path if the Supabase form is unavailable or not configured.

## Success criteria

- A visitor can understand who Riel is, see what he has built, open a live project, and contact him without hunting.
- All 16 supplied project links and descriptions are represented accurately.
- The experience feels crafted and motion-rich without obscuring content or harming usability.
